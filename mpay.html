MPay

2024 · Teletic

A secure P2P money transfer and bill payment platform, designed end-to-end for Banque Al Baraka Algeria across mobile and web — built to make everyday payments feel instant, trustworthy, and simple.

Overview

MPay unifies P2P transfers and everyday bill payments into a single, secure product for Banque Al Baraka Algeria's customers, replacing a fragmented set of in-branch and legacy-app workflows.

Problem

Customers juggled separate flows — often separate apps — to send money versus pay bills, with inconsistent verification steps that eroded trust at exactly the moment money was changing hands.

Goals

Cut the time to complete a transfer, unify transfers and bill payments under one mental model, and meet compliance requirements without the flow feeling like a form.

My Role

Lead Product Designer — end-to-end UX and UI, working closely with the Project Manager and CEO to keep the product aligned with compliance requirements and the launch timeline.

Process

Starting from the moments people trust least.

People didn't distrust the bank — they distrusted the moment of sending money. Process started there.

  • 14 customer interviews across two cities, focused on their last transfer.

  • Mapped the old multi-app journey to cut redundant steps.

  • Tested low-fi flows with branch staff before any visual design.

  • Weekly syncs with the PM and CEO to protect the timeline.

UX Decisions

A few decisions that mattered more than they looked.

  • Progressive disclosure replaces a five-step verification wall.

  • One consistent card state for pending, cleared, and failed.

  • Bills and transfers share a single "recipient" pattern.

  • Press-and-hold confirms sends — no extra screen needed.
