Fintech Bill Payments Financial Services

MPay

2024 · Teletic

A secure P2P transfer and bill payment platform for Banque Al Baraka Algeria. Built to make everyday payments feel instant and trustworthy.

Project Summary
Overview

One secure app for transfers and bill payments — replacing a fragmented mix of branch visits and legacy tools.

Problem

Separate flows for transfers and bills, with inconsistent verification that eroded trust exactly when money moved.

Goals

Faster transfers, one mental model for payments, and compliance that doesn't feel like paperwork.

My Role

Lead Product Designer, end-to-end — partnering closely with the PM and CEO on compliance and timeline.

Process

Starting from the moments people trust least.

People didn't distrust the bank — they distrusted the moment of sending money. Process started there.

  • 14 customer interviews across two cities, focused on their last transfer.

  • Mapped the old multi-app journey to cut redundant steps.

  • Tested low-fi flows with branch staff before any visual design.

  • Weekly syncs with the PM and CEO to protect the timeline.

Design System

One token set, two surfaces.

One token set for mobile and web — a single change updates every surface.

UX Decisions

A few decisions that mattered more than they looked.

  • Progressive disclosure replaces a five-step verification wall.

  • One consistent card state for pending, cleared, and failed.

  • Bills and transfers share a single "recipient" pattern.

  • Press-and-hold confirms sends — no extra screen needed.

Challenges

Designing inside a bank's compliance constraints.

Every money-touching screen needed compliance sign-off — some steps couldn't be cut. So required steps were explained briefly instead of hidden, keeping both compliance and usability intact.
